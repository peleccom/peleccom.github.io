--- layout: single title: "Privacy Policy — GTA 6 Countdown (Unofficial)" permalink: /privacy/gta6-countdown/ author_profile: false classes: wide sitemap: false description: >- Privacy policy for the unofficial GTA 6 Countdown Android app. --- _Last updated: October 2026_ This is an unofficial, fan-made countdown app for Grand Theft Auto VI. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. ## Data we collect We do **not** collect, sell, or share any personal data ourselves. We do not have accounts, analytics, or crash reporters. ## Advertising The app displays advertisements served by **Mintegral** (MBridge Android SDK). The ad SDK may automatically collect and process: - Device identifiers and advertising identifiers (Advertising ID) - IP address and coarse location derived from IP - Device and app information (model, OS version, language) - Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) The app does not show a consent dialog, so the SDK is initialized with GDPR consent **off** and Do-Not-Track **on**, meaning only non-personalized ads are served. This data is processed by Mintegral for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention, under Mintegral's own policies: [https://www.mintegral.com/en/privacy](https://www.mintegral.com/en/privacy) You can limit ad personalization via your device settings (Advertising ID / Opt-out of Ads Personalization). ## Local storage Streaks, theme choice, reminder settings, and ad-free entitlements are stored only on your device (SharedPreferences). You can clear them by uninstalling the app. ## Notifications Reminders and milestone alerts are scheduled locally on your device. No data leaves the device for this feature. ## Children The app is not directed at children under 13. ## Changes We may update this policy occasionally. The latest version always lives at this page.
